Loop Cuts

Overview

Loop cuts add new edge loops around your model (like adding rings around a cylinder) - press Ctrl+R to add more detail exactly where you need it!

Understanding Loop Cuts

Loop cuts are your precision tool for adding topology exactly where you need it. Rather than subdividing your entire mesh and adding complexity everywhere, loop cuts let you surgically insert new edge loops in specific locations.

What is a loop cut? An edge loop is a connected sequence of edges that forms a complete loop around your mesh. A loop cut creates a new edge loop that circles around your geometry.

To create loop cuts:

1. Enter Edit Mode and press Ctrl+R
2. Hover over an edge - you'll see a pink preview line loop around your model
3. Scroll your mouse wheel to add multiple loops at once
4. Click to place the loop(s), then move your mouse to slide them into position
5. Click again to confirm (or press Enter)

Strategic Topology: Loop cuts are essential for controlling where your mesh can deform. When animating characters, you need extra loops at joints (elbows, knees) to allow proper bending. For hard surface modeling, loop cuts help you define where edges should be sharp versus smooth.

What You're Seeing

In the visualization above, you can see a cylinder with multiple horizontal edge loops shown in green wireframe. Each of these rings represents a loop cut. Notice how they provide evenly-spaced control over the cylinder's form without adding unnecessary complexity to other areas.

Think of loop cuts like the growth rings in a tree or the latitude lines on a globe - they add detail in a structured, predictable way. This is much cleaner than randomly adding edges, which can create messy topology.

Why Loop Cuts Matter

Loop cuts maintain good topology (the flow of edges) while adding detail. Good topology is crucial for both modeling (it affects how tools like subdivision work) and animation (it determines how your model deforms).

You'll use loop cuts constantly when modeling organic forms, preparing models for animation, or anywhere you need localized detail without affecting the rest of your mesh.
