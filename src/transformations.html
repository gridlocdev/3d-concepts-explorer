Transformations

Move, rotate and scale: the three ways to change where an object is, which way it faces, and how big it is.

  • Move (also Translate or Grab) changes position. Drag the selection, or drag one arrow of the move gizmo.
  • Rotate turns the object around its pivot point. Each ring of the rotate gizmo turns it around one axis.
  • Scale resizes from the pivot. Scaling all axes evenly keeps proportions; scaling one axis stretches or squashes.

Axis constraints

Constraining a transform to one axis (or a plane of two axes) makes it predictable: a move locked to X only slides left and right, however the mouse moves. Drag a single gizmo handle, or use your tool's axis key.

For exact values, type them in. The same transforms work on whole objects and on selected vertices, edges and faces inside a mesh.
