Transformations

Move, rotate and scale change where an object is, which way it faces and how big it is. They work on whole objects and on selected vertices, edges and faces.

Drag a model to turn it.

In your tool

  1. Select the object, or vertices, edges or faces inside a mesh.
  2. Pick the move, rotate or scale tool (or its shortcut) and drag a gizmo handle.
  3. Type a number to set an exact distance, angle or factor.
