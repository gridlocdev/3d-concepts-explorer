Extrude

Extrude pulls selected faces, edges or vertices outward and fills the gap with new, connected geometry.

This stepped shape is one cube extruded several times: extrude the top face up, scale it down a little, repeat. Every step stays joined to the one below, so the result is still a single mesh with no gaps.

  1. Switch to editing the mesh's components.
  2. Select a face, edge or vertex.
  3. Run Extrude and drag, or type a distance.

By default a face extrudes along its normal, straight out from the surface. Constrain it to an axis to extrude in a fixed direction instead, for example straight up for walls.

Faces, edges and vertices

Typical uses: limbs and branches pulled out of a body, walls and window frames in architecture, or any shape grown step by step from a cube.
