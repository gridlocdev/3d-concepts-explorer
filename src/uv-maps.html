UV Maps

A UV map lays the surface of a model out flat, so a 2D image can be painted on it and wrapped back around.

Drag a model to turn it.

In your tool

  1. Mark seams where the mesh may be cut, along hidden or natural edges.
  2. Unwrap: the tool lays the pieces out flat.
  3. Check a checker texture on the model for stretching, then paint or apply textures.
