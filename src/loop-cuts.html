Loop Cuts

A loop cut inserts a new ring of edges all the way around a mesh, adding detail in one place without touching the rest.

An edge loop is a chain of edges that runs around the mesh and closes on itself, like the latitude lines on a globe. The rings on this cylinder are loops; each one is a place the shape can now bend or change width.

  1. Run the loop cut tool (also Insert Edge Loop, Swift Loop or Connect).
  2. Hover an edge to preview where the loop will run.
  3. Click to place it, then slide it into position.

Topology

Topology is how the edges flow across a mesh. Loops that follow the form deform cleanly when animated and subdivide cleanly. Edges added at random create triangles and n-gons (faces with more than four sides) that pinch and crease.

For animation, put extra loops at joints such as elbows and knees. For hard-surface models, loops close to an edge keep it sharp under subdivision.
