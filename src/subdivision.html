Subdivision Surface

Subdivision surface splits every face into four and smooths the result. You shape a simple cage; the tool generates the smooth surface from it.

Drag a model to turn it.

In your tool

  1. Add a subdivision surface modifier (or turn on smooth preview).
  2. Set a low level for the viewport and a higher one for rendering.
  3. Add support loops or crease edges that should stay sharp.
