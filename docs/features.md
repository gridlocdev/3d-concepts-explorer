# Features

3D Concepts Explorer explains the core ideas of 3D modeling, each shown with live 3D models. The ideas are the same in every modeling tool, so the pages explain the concept rather than one tool's buttons.

![The Materials page: the sidebar lists every concept by group, and the page shows a row of blue spheres going from glossy to matte as roughness rises from 0 to 1, an orange sphere as non-metal and as metal, and more spheres below for color, glass and emission](images/materials.webp)

## The concepts

The pages follow the order you work in: build a shape, refine it, give it a surface, then light and frame the scene.

- **Basics**: **Meshes** (vertices, edges and faces) and **Transformations** (move, rotate, scale and pivots).
- **Modeling**: **Extrude**, **Loop Cuts** and **Bevel**.
- **Smoothing**: **Normals**, and **Subdivision Surface**, which smooths a blocky cage.
- **Surface**: **Materials**, **UV Maps** and **Shaders**.
- **Scene**: **Lighting**, and **Camera** with its lenses and projections.

## Concept pages

Each page opens with a one-line summary of the concept, then a spread of small 3D models, one per idea, each with a short caption that says what it shows. Pages on modeling operations (Transformations, Extrude, Loop Cuts, Bevel, Subdivision Surface and UV Maps) end with **In your tool**: short steps for doing it in a modeling tool. Previous and next links at the bottom follow the sidebar order.

## Live models

Most models turn slowly on their own, and you can drag them to turn them yourself. A few, such as some of the camera views, stay still. Only the models on screen are drawn, so long pages stay smooth.

## Expanded view

The **Expand** button in a model's corner opens it large in a window over the page, with its title, its caption and its place in the page (for example `2 / 6`). The previous and next buttons, or the arrow keys, step through the page's models; **Close** or `Esc` goes back to the page. On a touch screen the **Expand** button is always shown; otherwise it shows when you point at the model.

## Fast page changes

Clicking a link to another page swaps the page in place, so the sidebar stays where it is and the new page's models start without a blank flash.

## Phones

On a narrow screen the sidebar moves to the top of the page and the models stack in one column.
